About My Technical Journey

I am a Senior Software Architect with over 25 years of expertise in building mission-critical systems. My journey is driven by Epistemology—a deep desire to understand the logic and first principles behind every line of code.

Research Philosophy: "To secure the future of AI, we must master its past." From auditing JDK source code to deriving neural gradients, I believe technical depth is the only path to architectural stability.

My passion for semantic understanding began in early search engine engineering and information retrieval (IR) systems:

  • Be-Bee USA (Personalization Tech): Dispatched from Be-Bee Taiwan to US HQ in Silicon Valley to pioneer personalized recommendation and user profiling algorithms.
  • NTU Library (Full-Text Retrieval): Developed high-performance full-text search engine systems for National Taiwan University Library's digital archives.

🔬 Deep Learning R&D: The IRNN Foundation

During my Master's research, I focused on the "Vanishing Gradient" problem, a fundamental hurdle in recurrent architectures. Instead of relying on high-level frameworks, I implemented Identity Recurrent Neural Networks (IRNN) from scratch in Java.

  • Mathematical Derivation: Hand-coded BPTT (Backpropagation Through Time) logic.
  • Stabilization: Leveraged identity matrix initialization to preserve signal flow across ultra-long sequences.
  • Legacy: This research laid the groundwork for my understanding of "Identity Drift" in complex data systems.

🌐 Neuro-Symbolic AI: HowNet Manifold Retrofitting

In our latest R&D, we bridge symbolic ontology with continuous vector spaces to eliminate statistical co-occurrence bias in dense embeddings (e.g., BGE-large-zh). By constraining neural vectors with HowNet 2,089 sememe graphs via Faruqui convex optimization, we achieved a statistically significant separation ($p = 0.000080$) between genuine conceptual similarity ($Q_1 = 0.9608$) and topical relatedness ($Q_2 = 0.6427$).

🛡️ Modern Challenge: Responsible AI & SQL-Guard

Today, I apply that same "First-Principles" rigor to Generative AI. My project, LLM-SQL-Guard, is a direct evolution of my work in sequence logic. Combined with Outlines FSM guided generation, it serves as a multi-layer security boundary ensuring AI-generated queries are safe, accurate, and schema-aligned.
